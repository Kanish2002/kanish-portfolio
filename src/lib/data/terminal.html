How Spatial Mathematics Shaped Low-Latency Architecture

Before tuning financial order matching engines, I spent two and a half years working with raw sensor depth arrays on the Azure Kinect DK. Processing millions of 3D spatial points per second in real time with Python, pyKinectAzure, and OpenCV instilled a fundamental truth: compute latency is conquered by minimizing allocations, streaming continuously, and honoring hardware caches.

This research culminated in the published paper: “Augmented Reality Sandbox With Play Based Learning For Preschool Children”, presenting novel approaches to interactive tactile topographical mapping with sub-30ms visual feedback loops.
